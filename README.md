## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Cybersecurity Policy is to establish the principles, responsibilities, standards, and procedures required to protect **AMATECH's information systems, networks, applications, devices, data, and users** from cybersecurity threats.

This policy aims to:

* Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of AMATECH's information and information systems.
* Reduce the likelihood and impact of cybersecurity incidents.
* Establish clear cybersecurity responsibilities for employees, contractors, and third parties.
* Promote secure and responsible use of AMATECH's technology resources.
* Support compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and industry requirements.
* Provide a framework for identifying, assessing, managing, and monitoring cybersecurity risks.
* Establish requirements for detecting, reporting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

* All AMATECH employees, management, interns, contractors, consultants, and temporary workers.
* Third parties and service providers who access AMATECH systems or information.
* All AMATECH-owned or authorized information systems and devices.
* Servers, workstations, laptops, mobile devices, network equipment, cloud services, applications, databases, and communication systems.
* Company information regardless of whether it is stored electronically, physically, or in cloud environments.
* Remote and hybrid working environments.

Anyone granted access to AMATECH information or technology resources is required to comply with this policy.

---

# 3. Cybersecurity Principles

AMATECH shall operate its cybersecurity program according to the following principles:

### 3.1 Confidentiality

Information shall only be accessed, disclosed, or used by authorized individuals and systems.

### 3.2 Integrity

Information and systems shall be protected against unauthorized modification, destruction, or manipulation.

### 3.3 Availability

Critical information and technology services shall be available to authorized users when required.

### 3.4 Least Privilege

Users shall receive only the level of access necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

### 3.5 Defense in Depth

AMATECH shall use multiple complementary security controls to reduce reliance on a single security mechanism.

### 3.6 Risk-Based Security

Cybersecurity controls shall be prioritized according to the likelihood and potential impact of identified risks.

### 3.7 Continuous Improvement

AMATECH shall periodically review cybersecurity controls, incidents, vulnerabilities, and emerging threats to improve its security posture.

# 4. Roles and Responsibilities

## 4.1 Executive Management

Executive Management shall:

* Provide appropriate support and resources for cybersecurity.
* Approve the cybersecurity policy.
* Establish organizational expectations regarding cybersecurity.
* Review significant cybersecurity risks and incidents.
* Ensure cybersecurity responsibilities are assigned appropriately.

## 4.2 Cybersecurity / IT Department

The Cybersecurity/IT Department shall:

* Implement and maintain cybersecurity controls.
* Monitor networks, systems, and security events.
* Conduct vulnerability assessments and security reviews.
* Manage cybersecurity incidents.
* Maintain security documentation.
* Provide cybersecurity awareness training.
* Maintain appropriate security configurations.
* Coordinate risk assessments and remediation activities.
* Monitor emerging cybersecurity threats.

## 4.3 Department Managers

Managers shall:

* Ensure employees comply with cybersecurity requirements.
* Approve access to systems based on business requirements.
* Review user access periodically.
* Report suspected security incidents.
* Ensure staff receive appropriate cybersecurity training.

## 4.4 Employees and Authorized Users

Users shall:

* Protect their credentials and company devices.
* Follow AMATECH cybersecurity procedures.
* Use company systems responsibly.
* Report suspicious activity or security incidents immediately.
* Avoid unauthorized software, applications, devices, or services.
* Complete required cybersecurity awareness training.

## 4.5 Third Parties

Third-party organizations with access to AMATECH systems or information shall comply with applicable contractual and cybersecurity requirements.

# 5. Acceptable Use Policy

AMATECH technology resources shall primarily be used for legitimate business purposes.

Users shall not:

* Access systems or information without authorization.
* Attempt to bypass security controls.
* Introduce malware or other malicious software.
* Conduct unauthorized penetration testing or security scanning.
* Use company systems to conduct illegal activities.
* Share passwords or authentication credentials.
* Install unauthorized software.
* Use company resources to distribute malicious, fraudulent, discriminatory, or offensive content.
* Connect unauthorized devices to protected company networks.

Limited personal use may be permitted where authorized and where it does not interfere with business operations or create security risks.

---

# 6. Identity and Access Management

AMATECH shall implement appropriate access controls to ensure that only authorized users can access company systems and information.

### Requirements

* Each user shall have a unique account.
* User accounts shall not be shared.
* Access shall follow the principle of least privilege.
* Privileged accounts shall be restricted to authorized personnel.
* Access shall be reviewed periodically.
* Access shall be removed promptly when employment or business requirements end.
* Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be implemented for critical systems where technically feasible.
* Administrative accounts shall be separated from standard user accounts where appropriate.

---

# 7. Password and Authentication Policy

Users shall:

* Use strong and unique passwords.
* Never share passwords with other individuals.
* Avoid using easily guessed information such as names, birthdays, or common words.
* Use an approved password manager where provided.
* Enable MFA when required.
* Report suspected credential compromise immediately.

Where technically feasible, AMATECH shall implement centralized authentication and appropriate password policies.

---

# 8. Data Protection and Classification

AMATECH information shall be classified according to its sensitivity and business importance.

A recommended classification scheme is:

| Classification   | Description                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Public**       | Information approved for public disclosure                         |
| **Internal**     | Information intended for authorized AMATECH personnel              |
| **Confidential** | Sensitive business, employee, customer, or operational information |
| **Restricted**   | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access controls      |

Sensitive information shall be protected through appropriate access controls, encryption, secure storage, and controlled transmission.

Users shall not transmit confidential or restricted information through unauthorized platforms.

---

# 9. Data Encryption

AMATECH shall use appropriate encryption mechanisms to protect sensitive information.

Encryption should be applied where appropriate to:

* Sensitive data stored on devices and servers.
* Data transmitted over untrusted networks.
* Backups containing sensitive information.
* Remote connections to company systems.

Cryptographic keys and credentials shall be protected from unauthorized access.

---

# 10. Endpoint Security

All company-managed endpoints shall have appropriate security controls.

Where applicable, devices shall use:

* Endpoint protection/antimalware solutions.
* Host-based firewalls.
* Automatic security updates.
* Secure configurations.
* Screen locks.
* Disk encryption.
* Centralized management.
* Security monitoring.

Users shall not disable security software or security controls without authorization.

---

# 11. Network Security

AMATECH shall implement controls to protect its network infrastructure.

These controls may include:

* Firewalls.
* Network segmentation.
* Secure wireless configurations.
* VPNs for approved remote access.
* Intrusion detection/prevention mechanisms where appropriate.
* Network monitoring.
* Secure DNS services.
* Access control lists.
* Regular review of network configurations.

Guest and untrusted devices should be isolated from sensitive internal resources where technically feasible.

---

# 12. Vulnerability Management

AMATECH shall maintain a vulnerability management process for identifying, assessing, prioritizing, and remediating vulnerabilities.

The process shall include:

1. Asset identification.
2. Vulnerability scanning.
3. Vulnerability validation.
4. Risk assessment.
5. Prioritization.
6. Remediation or mitigation.
7. Verification.
8. Documentation and reporting.

Critical vulnerabilities affecting internet-facing or business-critical systems shall receive priority based on risk and available remediation options.

---

# 13. Patch Management

Security updates shall be applied to supported systems within timeframes appropriate to their risk.

AMATECH shall:

* Maintain an inventory of systems and software.
* Monitor vendor security advisories.
* Test patches where appropriate.
* Prioritize critical security updates.
* Document patching activities.
* Address unsupported or end-of-life software.

Exceptions shall be documented and approved by an authorized individual.

---

# 14. Malware Protection

AMATECH shall deploy appropriate malware protection and prevention mechanisms.

Users shall:

* Avoid opening suspicious attachments or links.
* Avoid downloading software from untrusted sources.
* Report suspected malware infections.
* Not disable endpoint protection.
* Follow security warnings and alerts.

Security teams shall investigate suspected malware infections and take appropriate containment and remediation actions.

---

# 15. Email and Phishing Security

Email is a significant cybersecurity attack vector. AMATECH shall implement appropriate measures to reduce phishing and email-based threats.

Users shall:

* Verify suspicious requests for credentials, money, or sensitive information.
* Avoid clicking suspicious links.
* Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
* Report suspected phishing messages.
* Never provide passwords in response to unsolicited requests.

AMATECH may conduct authorized phishing-awareness exercises to improve employee awareness.

---

# 16. Security Monitoring and Logging

AMATECH shall maintain appropriate logging and monitoring for systems based on business and security requirements.

Logs may include:

* Authentication events.
* Failed login attempts.
* Administrative activities.
* Security alerts.
* Network activity.
* System changes.
* Application events.

Security logs shall be protected against unauthorized modification and retained according to applicable requirements.

---

# 17. Incident Response

AMATECH shall maintain an incident response process for identifying, reporting, analyzing, containing, eradicating, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

### Incident Response Lifecycle

**1. Preparation**
Establish policies, tools, contacts, procedures, and training.

**2. Detection and Analysis**
Identify and investigate suspicious activity.

**3. Containment**
Limit the spread and impact of the incident.

**4. Eradication**
Remove malicious activity and address the underlying cause.

**5. Recovery**
Restore affected systems safely and monitor for recurrence.

**6. Lessons Learned**
Document findings and improve security controls.

Employees shall report suspected cybersecurity incidents immediately to the designated IT/Cybersecurity team.

Examples of incidents include:

* Malware infections.
* Phishing attacks.
* Unauthorized access.
* Data breaches.
* Lost or stolen company devices.
* Credential compromise.
* Suspicious network activity.
* Denial-of-service attacks.
* Unauthorized disclosure of information.

---

# 18. Backup and Recovery

AMATECH shall maintain appropriate backups for critical systems and information.

Backups shall:

* Be performed according to business requirements.
* Be protected from unauthorized access.
* Be tested periodically.
* Include appropriate retention periods.
* Be protected against accidental deletion and, where feasible, ransomware-related compromise.
* Support recovery objectives for critical business services.

---

# 19. Security Awareness and Training

All employees shall receive cybersecurity awareness training appropriate to their roles.

Training should cover:

* Password security.
* Phishing awareness.
* Social engineering.
* Malware.
* Safe internet usage.
* Data protection.
* Incident reporting.
* Remote working security.
* Acceptable use of company resources.

Employees with specialized responsibilities may receive additional technical security training.

---

# 20. Remote Working and Mobile Security

Employees working remotely shall:

* Use approved devices and applications.
* Connect through approved secure mechanisms.
* Avoid accessing sensitive information over insecure public networks without appropriate protection.
* Secure company devices from unauthorized access.
* Lock devices when unattended.
* Report lost or stolen equipment immediately.

Company information shall not be stored on unauthorized personal devices or services.

---

# 21. Physical Security

AMATECH shall implement physical security controls appropriate to the sensitivity of its facilities and systems.

Controls may include:

* Restricted access to server rooms.
* Visitor management.
* Physical access controls.
* CCTV where appropriate.
* Secure disposal of sensitive documents.
* Protection of networking equipment.
* Environmental controls for critical infrastructure.

Unauthorized persons shall not be permitted to access restricted areas without appropriate authorization.

---

# 22. Third-Party and Supplier Security

Third parties that process AMATECH information or access company systems shall be assessed according to the risks they introduce.

Where appropriate, contracts shall include requirements relating to:

* Confidentiality.
* Data protection.
* Access control.
* Security incident notification.
* Vulnerability management.
* Secure handling of company information.
* Termination of access.
* Compliance with applicable security requirements.

Third-party access shall be limited to the minimum necessary.

---

# 23. Security Testing

AMATECH may conduct authorized:

* Vulnerability assessments.
* Security configuration reviews.
* Penetration tests.
* Phishing simulations.
* Network security assessments.
* Incident response exercises.

Security testing shall be properly authorized and documented before execution.

Unauthorized security testing against AMATECH systems is prohibited.

---

# 24. Change Management

Changes to critical IT systems and security infrastructure shall follow an appropriate change-management process.

Changes should be:

* Requested and documented.
* Assessed for security and operational impact.
* Tested where appropriate.
* Approved by authorized personnel.
* Implemented in a controlled manner.
* Documented after implementation.

Emergency changes shall be reviewed retrospectively.

---

# 25. Asset Management

AMATECH shall maintain an inventory of relevant hardware, software, systems, and information assets.

The inventory should identify, where appropriate:

* Asset owner.
* Asset type.
* Location.
* Business importance.
* Responsible department.
* Security requirements.
* Lifecycle status.

Assets that are no longer required shall be securely decommissioned or disposed of.

---

# 26. Risk Management

AMATECH shall maintain a cybersecurity risk management process.

Risk assessments shall consider:

* Threats.
* Vulnerabilities.
* Likelihood.
* Business impact.
* Existing controls.
* Residual risk.
* Risk treatment options.

Risk treatment may include:

* Mitigation.
* Avoidance.
* Transfer.
* Acceptance.

Significant cybersecurity risks shall be communicated to appropriate management.

---

# 27. Security Documentation

AMATECH shall maintain appropriate cybersecurity documentation, including:

* Policies.
* Procedures.
* Network diagrams.
* Asset inventories.
* Incident reports.
* Vulnerability reports.
* Risk assessments.
* Access reviews.
* Security configurations.
* Business continuity and recovery documentation.

Documentation shall be reviewed and updated periodically.

---

# 28. Compliance and Legal Requirements

AMATECH shall comply with applicable cybersecurity, privacy, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

Where applicable, cybersecurity practices should take into consideration recognized frameworks and standards such as:

* **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)**
* **ISO/IEC 27001**
* **CIS Controls**
* **MITRE ATT&CK**
* **OWASP security guidance**

The specific legal and regulatory requirements applicable to AMATECH shall be identified and maintained by the appropriate responsible functions.

---

# 29. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

* Additional cybersecurity training.
* Removal or restriction of system access.
* Disciplinary action.
* Contractual consequences for third parties.
* Legal action where appropriate.

The response to a violation shall depend on its nature, severity, intent, and applicable organizational procedures and law.

---

# 30. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy shall:

1. Be formally documented.
2. Include a business justification.
3. Identify associated security risks.
4. Define compensating controls where appropriate.
5. Receive approval from the designated authority.
6. Include an expiration or review date where appropriate.

---

# 31. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever there are significant changes to:

* AMATECH's technology environment.
* Business operations.
* Cybersecurity risks.
* Applicable laws or regulations.
* Organizational structure.
* Major cybersecurity incidents.

Updates shall be approved through the organization's established governance process.
